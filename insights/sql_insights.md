Revenue Concentration

• Top 20% of products generate most of the revenue → revenue risk is concentrated → expand high-performing product lines or reduce dependency.

Repeat Customers

• Repeat customers place multiple orders and contribute higher revenue → retention is strong → invest in loyalty programs and personalized offers.

Category Performance

• One category dominates sales but shows lower profit margins → pricing or discount strategy may be inefficient → review discounts and supplier costs.

Regional Performance

• One region outperforms others in revenue → regional demand varies → allocate more marketing and inventory to high-performing regions.

Monthly Trend

• Certain months show consistent revenue spikes → seasonal demand exists → plan inventory and promotions around peak months.
