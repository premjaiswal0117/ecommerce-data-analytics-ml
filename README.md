# E-commerce Data Analytics & Customer Segmentation

## Problem Statement
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify revenue drivers, profitability issues, and customer purchasing behavior. It also applies machine learning to segment customers for targeted business strategies.

## Tech Stack
- SQL (MySQL)
- Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)
- Machine Learning (K-Means Clustering)
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Analysis
- Analyzed revenue trends across time, categories, and regions
- Identified customer purchasing patterns and repeat behavior
- Evaluated profitability and pricing inefficiencies
- Assessed impact of discounts on profit

## Machine Learning (Customer Segmentation)
Applied K-Means clustering to segment customers based on sales, profit, and quantity.

- High-value customers → high revenue and profit contribution  
- Medium-value customers → potential for upselling  
- Low-value customers → low engagement, require targeted marketing  

This segmentation helps businesses prioritize retention and growth strategies.

## Key Business Insights
- High revenue does not always translate into high profit, indicating pricing inefficiencies  
- A small group of customers contributes a significant portion of revenue  
- Discount has limited impact on profit, suggesting pricing flexibility  
- Certain products generate high sales but low profit, requiring optimization  

## Business Impact
- Focus retention strategies on high-value customers  
- Improve pricing strategy for low-margin products  
- Use targeted marketing for customer segmentation
