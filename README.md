\# E-commerce Sales \& Customer Analytics



\## 📌 Problem Statement



This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify revenue drivers, profitability issues, and customer purchasing behavior. The goal is to support business decision-making through data-driven insights.



\---



\## 🛠️ Tech Stack



\* SQL (MySQL)

\* Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib)

\* Jupyter Notebook



\---



\## 📊 Key Analysis



\* Revenue analysis (monthly trends, category-wise, region-wise)

\* Customer behavior analysis (repeat customers, top customers)

\* Profitability analysis (profit margin, high sales but low profit products)

\* Discount impact analysis



\---



\## 💡 Key Business Insights



\* High revenue does not always translate into high profit, indicating pricing or cost inefficiencies

\* A small group of repeat customers contributes a large portion of total revenue

\* Discount levels do not significantly impact profit margins, suggesting strong pricing flexibility or data limitations

\* Certain products generate high sales but low profit, requiring pricing or cost optimization
